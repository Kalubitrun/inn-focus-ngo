Donor information

Before you contribute

Is my donation eligible for tax benefit?

Yes, subject to Income Tax provisions. The organisation is registered under Section 12A and approved under Section 80G. After payment, share your name, amount and PAN on WhatsApp at +91 91706 94591 and we will issue the applicable receipt.

How do I pay by UPI?

Open any UPI app (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM), scan the QR on this page or pay to 9170694591@ybl. Then complete the receipt form above so we can identify and acknowledge your payment.

How are funds used?

Funds support INN Focus Classes in Gahmar — teaching support, books and learning materials, girl-child retention efforts, skill sessions and community awareness. Administrative overhead is kept minimal.

Can I transfer directly to the bank account?

Yes. Use “Request bank details on WhatsApp” above. Account details of our Union Bank of India account are shared after basic verification, and a receipt is issued on confirmation of credit.
