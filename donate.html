Donor Questions

Before You Donate

Is my donation tax-exempt? (12A / 80G)

Yes. INN Focus NGO is 12A registered and 80G approved under the Income Tax Act. After donation, share your name + amount + PAN (for 80G) on WhatsApp 9170694591 and we send the receipt.

How do I donate via UPI?

1. Open PhonePe / GPay / Paytm / BHIM → 2. Scan the QR on this page or pay to UPI ID 9170694591@ybl → 3. Fill the receipt form above so we thank you properly.

Where will my money go?

Directly to INN Focus Classes, Gahmar — books, classes for Class 1-10, girl child support, skills and health drives. No big office cost.

Can I donate without UPI? Bank transfer?

Yes. Click “Get Bank Details on WhatsApp” above. We share Union Bank of India account details on request.
