What we do

Six focus areas, one standard of care

All programmes operate from our base in Gahmar, with low overhead and direct delivery.

Education Support

Free after-school support for Classes 1–10, including homework help and exam preparation.

Skill Development

Communication, basic computer use and vocational guidance for self-reliance.

Community Welfare

Awareness sessions on health, rights and schooling, with support in genuine emergencies.

Environment

Plantation and cleanliness drives for a greener, healthier village.

Women and Child Empowerment

Admission support, safety awareness and learning support with emphasis on the girl child.

Health and Hygiene

Hygiene, nutrition and preventive-health guidance for children and mothers.
