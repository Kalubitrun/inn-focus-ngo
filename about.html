Who We Are

A Grassroots NGO from Ghazipur, UP

INN FOCUS NGO – Educational Empowerment Society is a non-governmental organization working for Education, Skill Development and Social Empowerment.

Based at Vill-Khudura, Post-Gahmar, Distt Ghazipur, Uttar Pradesh, we run INN Focus Classes and community programs so underprivileged children — especially girls — get quality learning, life skills and equal opportunity.

  • ✅ Education support for Classes 1 to 10
  • ✅ Life skills, vocational skills & values
  • ✅ Girl child education & women empowerment
  • ✅ Health, hygiene, environment & community awareness

“Education is not just a right, it’s a tool for a better tomorrow.”

INN Focus Classes, Gahmar — our real students

Why We Exist

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯 Our Mission

To provide quality education, skill development and equal opportunities to underprivileged children and communities, so that they can build a better and brighter future.

🌱 Our Vision

An educated, empowered and self-reliant society where every child gets a chance to grow, learn and achieve their dreams.

💛 Our Values

DREAM → LEARN → GROW → SUCCEED.
Quality education for all, empowered communities, self-reliant future — together we grow.
