Who we are

A community organisation, run locally

INN Focus NGO — Educational Empowerment Society is a registered non-profit working in education, skill development and social empowerment.

From our centre at Vill. Khudura, Post Gahmar, District Ghazipur, we operate INN Focus Classes and village outreach programmes. Our priority is children who risk dropping out — particularly girls — and youth who need practical skills to earn.

  • Structured support for Classes 1–10, aligned to school curriculum
  • Life skills, study discipline and basic vocational orientation
  • Girl-child enrolment, retention and confidence-building
  • Health, hygiene, environment and community awareness

“Education is not only a right. It is the most reliable path to a better future.”
