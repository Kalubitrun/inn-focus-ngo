Small Support, Big Change

What Your Donation Supports

Every program runs from our base in Gahmar, Ghazipur — INN Focus Classes + village outreach. No big office cost. Direct help to children and families.

📖

Classes 1 to 10

1. Education Support

Free learning support, homework help and exam preparation for school children who cannot afford tuition.

  • ✅ Daily classes at INN Focus Classes
  • ✅ Books, guidance & moral values
  • ✅ Dropout prevention & parent meetings

Outcome: brighter future, no child left behind.

🛠️

Life & Vocational Skills

2. Skill Development

Practical skills for youth so they become self-reliant — not just degree holders.

  • ✅ Communication & life skills
  • ✅ Basic computer & vocational guidance
  • ✅ Career counselling for village youth

Outcome: self-reliance and growth.

👧

Beti Padhao

3. Girl Child Education & Empowerment

Special focus on girls — admission support, safety awareness and confidence building.

  • ✅ Extra support for girl students
  • ✅ Health, safety & dignity sessions
  • ✅ Women & mothers involvement

Outcome: equality and dignity.

🤝

Village Development

4. Community Development & Awareness

Village meetings, awareness drives and emergency help for families in need.

  • ✅ Education & rights awareness
  • ✅ Support during family emergencies
  • ✅ Together We Grow volunteers

Outcome: healthier and safer society.

5. Health & Hygiene Awareness

Clean habits, nutrition and basic health knowledge for children and mothers.

  • ✅ Handwash, cleanliness drives
  • ✅ Nutrition: “no one sleeps hungry”
  • ✅ Health check guidance

Outcome: better health for all.

Youth leadership, women self-help spirit and green village — plantation & cleanliness.

  • ✅ Environment: greenery & clean village
  • ✅ Social service: strong society, strong nation
